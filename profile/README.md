# CevicLabs

We build software solutions around how your work is actually done.

CevicLabs is a software company in Ireland. We build tools that do one job well, keep your data on your own device, and don't need an account.

## Products

**[Job Docket](https://ceviclabs.com/jobdocket/)**: jobs, time, parts and mileage for tradespeople, on your Android phone. No sign-up, nothing sent to the cloud. Questions and bug reports: [CevicLabs/jobdocket](https://github.com/CevicLabs/jobdocket).

## Research

**[HIQA compliance research](https://github.com/CevicLabs/hiqa-compliance-research)**: how care providers in Ireland keep track of their compliance documents, and where it gets hard.

## Open source

- [usb-disk-stop](https://github.com/CevicLabs/usb-disk-stop): spin down a USB disk whose enclosure ignores ATA standby.
- [ceviclabs-site](https://github.com/CevicLabs/ceviclabs-site): the source of ceviclabs.com. Plain HTML and CSS, no JavaScript, no tracking.
